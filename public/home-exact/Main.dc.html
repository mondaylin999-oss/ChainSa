HOW IT WORKS

How to use the Explore page

1

Choose an Earth system

Pick a category in the left panel, such as Agriculture or Water.

2

Set township, dataset, time

Use the three dropdowns: Township, Dataset (e.g. NDVI) and Time Period.

3

Read the map

Colors run from Low to High. Zoom, switch layers, or re-center on your township.

4

Switch the view

Tabs show the same data as Trend Analysis, Comparison, Map View or Data Table.

5

Check the trend

See the annual change, whether it is significant, plus temperature, rainfall and soil moisture.

6

Ask for guidance

AI Decision Support gives key insights and recommended actions. Open the detailed recommendation for more.
